XTRA Advance ESE Lightning Arrester

The Rudra XTRA Advance ESE Lightning Arrester is an Early Streamer Emission air terminal intended for engineered lightning protection systems in commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects.

  • XTRA Advance Early Streamer Emission product range
  • Advance time of 60 μs as stated on the product label
  • Label-stated protection radius of 107 m, subject to design verification
  • Model-specific technical documentation available on request
