Lightning Protection Monitoring

Lightning Strike Counter

Rudra Lightning Strike Counters are intended for monitoring lightning discharge events in engineered lightning protection systems. Recorded event counts support inspection and maintenance activities in industrial facilities and commercial infrastructure, subject to the selected model and installation configuration. Explore RES-DLSC0101 and RES-ALSC0102 catalogue models and request detection specifications, display information, availability and quotations.
