Who we are

Earthing & Lightning Protection Manufacturer

Rudra Earthing Systems is a manufacturer and supplier of electrical earthing and lightning protection products based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. With manufacturing experience since 2017, our focus is on product quality, material selection and engineering support.

Our earthing product portfolio includes GI earthing electrodes, pure copper electrodes, copper bonded earthing electrodes, copper bonded grounding rods, ESE lightning arresters, conventional lightning arresters, solar earthing kits and grounding accessories. We support residential, commercial and industrial infrastructure requirements through product selection and project assistance.
