Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the handling of information through the Rudra Earthing Systems website and its associated product enquiry and quotation services.

rudraearthingsystem.com Rudra Earthing Systems Last updated: 27 November 2026
01

Introduction

Rudra Earthing Systems ("Rudra", "we", "our" or "us") operates rudraearthingsystem.com to provide information about earthing, grounding and lightning protection products and to facilitate business enquiries.

This policy applies to information collected when you browse our website, create a quotation list, submit an enquiry or communicate with our team through the contact channels provided on this website.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

02

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you submit a product quotation or business enquiry, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name.
  • Company or organisation name.
  • Telephone or mobile number.
  • Email address, where provided.
  • Project or delivery location.
  • Selected products and quantities.
  • Technical specifications and project requirements.
  • Messages and other information voluntarily provided by you.

Enquiry records

After a successful submission, an enquiry record may include a unique reference number, submission time, enquiry status and internal notes relevant to handling your request.

Technical information

Website hosting, security and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser characteristics, device information, request details and diagnostic logs.

Campaign information

If you arrive through a marketing campaign, our website may capture campaign identifiers, UTM parameters and Google Click Identifiers (GCLID) from the website address.

Please note: Our quotation form is intended for business and product requirements. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, Aadhaar numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website may be used for the following purposes:

  • Responding to product and business enquiries.
  • Preparing quotations and discussing product requirements.
  • Providing available technical information and documentation.
  • Communicating regarding supply, availability and delivery requirements.
  • Managing enquiry records and relevant customer communications.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing automated spam or misuse.
  • Diagnosing technical issues and improving website functionality.
  • Understanding the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Contact details submitted through a quotation request may be used to contact you about that request by telephone, email or other communication channels agreed with you.

04

Consent and Processing

Our online quotation form requires an affirmative consent selection before an enquiry can be submitted.

By providing the required information and submitting the form with your consent, you authorise processing of that information for handling your enquiry and related communication.

You may choose not to provide optional information. However, certain details, such as your name, contact number and enquiry requirements, are necessary for online enquiry processing.

Where applicable, information may also be processed as permitted or required by law.

You may contact us to withdraw consent for future processing based on your consent. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

Consent to respond to an enquiry is not automatically consent to unrelated promotional communications or optional advertising tracking.

05

Cookies & Browser Storage

Our website uses browser storage technologies to maintain quotation functionality and campaign attribution.

Website storage technologies
Storage Purpose Duration
Local Storage
rudra-rfq-v1
Stores selected products, product identifiers and quotation quantities. Until removed, cleared following successful submission, or deleted through browser settings.
Session Storage
rudra-attribution
Stores campaign parameters and advertising click identifiers. Generally for the browser tab session, subject to browser behaviour.
Security Technologies Support bot detection, abuse prevention and enquiry verification. Depends on the security service and configuration.
Analytics & Advertising May support website measurement and campaign reporting when enabled. Depends on the service, settings and applicable consent requirements.

Managing website storage

You can manage or delete website cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Clearing local storage may remove your saved quotation list.

Clearing browser storage does not automatically delete enquiries that have already been submitted to our backend.

Blocking certain security technologies may prevent successful submission of the online quotation form. You can alternatively contact our team by telephone or email.

07

Analytics & Advertising

Rudra Earthing Systems may use online advertising campaigns to promote its products and services.

Campaign attribution

Our website can retain certain campaign parameters associated with a website visit and include them with a submitted business enquiry.

  • Campaign source (utm_source).
  • Campaign medium (utm_medium).
  • Campaign name (utm_campaign).
  • Campaign keyword (utm_term).
  • Campaign content (utm_content).
  • Google Click Identifier (gclid).

Conversion measurement

The website is designed to make a successful enquiry event available to a configured measurement data layer after backend confirmation.

Where Google Ads, Google Analytics or similar measurement services are enabled, they may process campaign identifiers, website interactions, conversion events, cookies and related technical information.

Such services should be configured with appropriate privacy disclosures and consent controls where required by applicable law or provider policies.

Advertising privacy choices

You can learn about Google's advertising and data practices, and manage available preferences, through the following services:

  • How Google Uses Information from Partner Sites
  • Google My Ad Center
  • How Google Uses Cookies
08

Information Sharing

Information may be shared where necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including with:

  • Authorised Rudra Earthing Systems personnel responsible for enquiries, sales and administration.
  • Hosting, database, security and technical service providers supporting website operations.
  • Service providers involved in fulfilling an agreed business requirement, where necessary.
  • Government authorities or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Enquiry information is intended for legitimate business processing and is not intended to be published publicly through the website.

09

Data Security

Our website and enquiry system are designed to use technical safeguards appropriate to their functionality.

These safeguards include:

  • HTTPS-based website communications.
  • Server-side enquiry validation.
  • Automated bot and spam verification.
  • Database access restrictions.
  • Role-based access for authorised enquiry staff.
  • Backend-managed service credentials.

Although reasonable safeguards are intended to reduce security risks, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please notify us if you become aware of a suspected security or privacy issue involving our website.

10

Data Retention

Enquiry and business records may be retained for handling customer requirements, maintaining business records, resolving disputes and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods depend on the nature of the enquiry, the business relationship, operational requirements and applicable legal obligations.

You may contact our team to request information about the retention or deletion of your personal information.

Information contained in service provider logs or backups may be subject to separate retention arrangements.

11

Your Privacy Choices

Subject to applicable law and relevant legal requirements, you may contact us regarding:

  • Access to personal information associated with your enquiries.
  • Correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure of personal information where appropriate.
  • Withdrawal of consent for processing based on consent.
  • Preferences regarding promotional communications, where applicable.
  • Questions, complaints or grievances regarding personal information.

Where applicable data protection law provides additional rights, those rights may be exercised in accordance with that law.

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity and locate the relevant enquiry before processing a privacy request.

If you have received an enquiry reference number, including it in your request may help us locate the relevant record.
