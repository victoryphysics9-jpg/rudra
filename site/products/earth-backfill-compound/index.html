Earth Backfill Compound

Rudra Earth Backfill Compound is an earthing compound and ground enhancement material designed to improve conductivity around earthing electrodes. It is available in 10, 20 and 25 kg catalogue pack sizes under RES-BFC10, RES-BFC20 and RES-BFC25 models for industrial, commercial and project-specific electrical grounding applications. Contact Rudra Earthing Systems for material specifications, pack availability and bulk quantity quotations.
