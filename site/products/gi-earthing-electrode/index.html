GI Earthing Electrode

Rudra GI Earthing Electrodes feature hot dip galvanized iron construction with a compound-filled configuration for electrical grounding and fault-current dissipation as part of a properly engineered earthing system. Available catalogue models include RES-GI40, RES-GI50 and RES-GI80 with length options of 1, 2 and 3 metres for industrial equipment earthing, commercial buildings and electrical infrastructure. Explore terminal dimensions, product specifications and request quotations from Rudra Earthing Systems, Lucknow, India.
