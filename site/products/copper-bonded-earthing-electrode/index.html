Copper Bonded Earthing Electrode

Rudra Copper Bonded Earthing Electrodes are designed for electrical grounding and fault-current dissipation as part of a properly engineered earthing system. Available in multiple models and length options for project-specific requirements.

  • Copper bonded electrode construction
  • Available in RES-CB40, RES-CB50 and RES-CB80 models
  • Length options: 1, 2 and 3 metres
  • Compound-filled configuration as listed in the catalogue
