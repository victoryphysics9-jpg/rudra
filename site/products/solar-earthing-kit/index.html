Solar Earthing Kit

Rudra Solar Earthing Kits are intended for electrical grounding applications in rooftop solar panel installations, ground-mounted solar power projects and photovoltaic infrastructure. The catalogue includes grounding rod diameter options of 14 and 17 mm with lengths of 1, 2 and 3 metres across multiple solar earthing kit models. Contact Rudra Earthing Systems for confirmed kit contents, rod specifications, availability and quotations for residential, commercial and industrial solar projects.

  • Designed for solar installation grounding
  • Catalogue rod diameters: 14 and 17 mm
  • Length options: 1, 2 and 3 metres
  • Multiple model configurations for project-specific selection
