Technical Knowledge

Earthing & Lightning Protection FAQs

Common questions about electrical grounding, earthing products and lightning protection systems.

What is an industrial earthing system?

An industrial earthing system connects designated electrical equipment and conductive parts to earth to support electrical safety and fault protection. The design depends on electrical system requirements, soil conditions and applicable standards.

Which earthing electrode is suitable for industrial applications?

GI, pure copper and copper bonded earthing electrodes are available for different grounding applications. Selection depends on soil conditions, corrosion risks, fault current requirements and engineering specifications. Explore our earthing product range for available options.

What products are used for solar earthing?

Solar grounding installations may use earthing rods, grounding conductors, connectors and other accessories specified by the project engineer. Explore our solar earthing kit and copper bonded earthing rods.

What is the difference between an ESE and conventional lightning arrester?

Conventional lightning protection uses air terminals designed to intercept lightning strikes. ESE air terminals use a different design approach and are covered by different standards. Selection should be based on applicable regulations, project specifications and qualified engineering assessment.
